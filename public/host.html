How to Play

  • Hold your phone sideways (right edge up) and tilt it left, right, up, down, to steer your blob
  • Bump NPC blobs to grow
  • Bump other players to grow even more
  • NPC blobs respawn as bombs — avoid their blast radius until they explode
  • First player to reach full jelly blob potential wins
  • Leaderboard keeps track of wins over the whole game session
